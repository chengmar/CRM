## Status Rules

- `IMPLEMENTED` requires source, additive migration or compatibility, independent behavior tests, an executable fixture/shadow result, an operator surface, a downstream metric path, and explicit risk.
- `VERIFIED` additionally requires the named tests and the complete test/typecheck/build gate to pass after the final integration.
- `BLOCKED_EXTERNAL` is used only where the local contract, disabled-by-default adapter, budget/idempotency controls, fixture, and shadow harness exist and the remaining live step requires unavailable external credentials/account state, live platform acceptance, or an authorization not already recorded.
- Synthetic HOLD results are never represented as production validation. A row can be technically implemented while its business decision remains HOLD.
